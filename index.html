Who We Are

Built for Real Chicago Move Days

Owner-operated by three longtime friends, with move planning shaped around Chicago buildings, streets, and stairways.

The practical details matter before the truck arrives. These are the items we ask customers to confirm with building management while the move is being scheduled.

COIs and building approval

If management requires a certificate of insurance, send its sample wording and deadline early so the documentation can be prepared before move day.

Elevators, docks, and loading access

We ask about reserved elevator windows, dock instructions, curb access, alleys, gangways, rear stairs, and the full carry route when planning the arrival.

Furniture protection and fit

Moving blankets, shrink wrap, and disassembly are planned around the item, doorway, turns, and stairs rather than waiting to discover a tight fit during the move.

Starting rate and quote details

Rates currently start at $150 per hour for two movers and a truck, with a two-hour minimum. Confirm travel time, materials, special-item work, and the expected crew size in your written quote.
