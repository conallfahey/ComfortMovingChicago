# Chicago Apartment Movers

Comfort Moving Chicago specializes in apartment and condo moves, from walk-ups to high-rises. The company sizes the crew and truck to the inventory, stairs, and loading access so customers can avoid booking more help than the move needs.

## Starting rate

Local apartment moves start at $135 per hour for two movers and a truck, with a two-hour minimum. Inventory, distance, building access, and extras such as packing affect the quote.

## Building access

Share the floor number and stair or elevator access at both addresses. Include any freight elevator reservation, loading dock instructions, certificate of insurance requirements, and bulky items that might need disassembly or hoisting.
