Check the Berwyn loading spot before you book

Start with the exact address and the side of the street where the truck would stop. Berwyn’s street maintenance map shows different schedules by block and notes that it is for reference. Read the signs at the actual space and, if a restriction overlaps your loading window, confirm a legal alternative or move the start time. A space across the street can mean a longer carry and a different plan for the crew.

Around the Depot District

The Berwyn Collector’s Office directs Depot District permit parking questions to the Police Department. Ask the building which entrance and loading space movers can use. Read the signs at the actual spot before treating a nearby lot or street space as a backup.

If a rental truck or moving van needs to stay overnight

For a split move, contact Berwyn Police before relying on overnight street parking. Berwyn Traffic Code § 480.03 describes a one-night exemption for rental trucks and moving vans with Police Department approval. Give them the vehicle type and address and confirm where it may stay.

Choose the route from the truck to your door

Walk the front route and any permitted rear route before move day. If the front door has porch steps or a tight turn inside, measure the narrowest point with the door open. For the rear route, check the alley approach, gate width, and turns between the garage and the door. A rear entrance can make the carry shorter, but only when there is legal loading access and the route stays clear for neighbors.

Take a photo from the planned stopping point toward the entrance, then another at the narrowest turn. For a sofa or a heavy piece, send the item dimensions and note if the legs can come off. For an apartment, confirm move hours and who holds the keys. If the building has an elevator, ask how to reserve it and whether it needs insurance documentation from the mover.

Berwyn moving FAQ

What if street cleaning falls on my move day?
Check the signs on both sides of your block. Confirm a legal loading spot outside the restricted area, or talk with your mover about shifting the start time. Share the change in advance if the alternative makes the carry longer.
Can I assume my visitor pass covers a moving truck overnight?
Confirm directly with Berwyn Police. Its parking page discusses visitor passes, while the city code addresses overnight rental trucks and moving vans separately. Explain your situation, including the address and vehicle type.
What if a sofa will only fit through the rear entrance?
Send the sofa dimensions and photos of the gate, door and turns. Confirm that you can use the rear entrance and that there is a legal place to load. If the only loading spot is at the front, show the path between that spot and the rear door so we can plan the carry.
